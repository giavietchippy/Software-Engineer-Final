=============================================

Lỗi "Trang bạn tìm kiếm không tồn tại" = URL VNPay sai HOẶC chưa có TmnCode + HashSecret.
Config hien tai TmnCode/HashSecret TRONG → khong the thanh toan that.

BUOC 1 — Dang ky mien phi (2 phut)
----------------------------------
1. Mo: https://sandbox.vnpayment.vn/devreg/
2. Dien email cua ban, gui form
3. Check email → copy:
   - Terminal / TmnCode (vd: ABCD1234)
   - Secret / HashSecret (chuoi dai)

BUOC 2 — Dan vao app (1 phut)
-----------------------------
Mo: SaigonBite.Web\appsettings.Development.json

  "Mode": "Live",
  "PublicBaseUrl": "https://localhost:7122",
  "VnPay": {
    "TmnCode": "DAN-TMN-TU-EMAIL",
    "HashSecret": "DAN-SECRET-TU-EMAIL",
    ...
  }

Luu → chay lai scripts\run-web.cmd
